# Jennys Glass & Aluminum — Section Plan

> Structure derived from the differentiators and the customer's decision journey —
> **not** the stock Hero → Services → About → Contact order (which is what the
> current build uses, and what the discovery exists to replace).

## Primary action
**Get a quote** — today via **Call** or **Facebook Messenger** (the owner will send
a quotation form); later, a system-handled quote request. Every section drives here
or supports the decision to do it.

## Order & rationale
1. **Opener — the accountable, established yard** (not a generic welcome). Leads with
   *why this yard*: eight years supplying, fabricating, and installing glass &
   aluminum across Antique — a real store, real permits, real people who answer for
   the work. — fills from: Cluster 1 (one-breath, origin, location/years) + Cluster 4
   Q14 (permits, receipts, physical store, established name) — action: **Call / Message
   for a quote**.
2. **What we do — supply · fabricate · install** — the range, plainly, so installers
   and homeowners both see themselves: materials (glass, aluminum, roll-up + accessories);
   fabrication (cabinets, shower enclosures, kitchen cabinets, roll-up slats);
   installation (glass, windows, doors, roll-up). — fills from: Cluster 2 Q4/Q5 —
   action: get a quote.
3. **Someone to answer for the work** *(bespoke — the differentiator)* — the legal
   framing of the real edge: a permitted, receipt-issuing, physical store that stands
   behind every job, so the customer always has a real business to contact and hold
   accountable — plus genuine aftercare and support the cheaper/closer option won't give.
   — fills from: Cluster 4 Q11/Q14 (accountability, aftercare, legitimacy) — action:
   get a quote.
4. **Built by a family, still here** *(bespoke)* — the origin (founded Feb 2017 to
   support the owner's children's education and care for employees) and the resilience
   note, framed forward: scaled back briefly in 2026, now back and moving ahead with
   determination. Humanizes "real people." Keep tasteful — do not dwell on the financial
   difficulty. — fills from: Cluster 1 (origin + recovery line) — action: secondary
   (read on / contact).
5. **How buying works** *(bespoke — concrete local trade mechanics)* — call/Messenger to
   check stock → visit the store or arrange delivery; 50% downpayment, balance within one
   week (subject to approval); cash; delivery province-wide, scheduled North/South days,
   min PHP 20,000 *(note: delivery currently being restored)*; hours 8 AM–5 PM, Mon–Sat.
   — fills from: Cluster 5 Q16/Q17/Q18/Q19 — action: get a quote / message.
6. **Get a quote** — the spine, frictionless: **Call for a Quote** + **Message on
   Messenger** now; a simple quote-request form (future: system-handled quotation). —
   fills from: Cluster 6 Q21 — action: **primary CTA**.
7. **Find us** — the physical store as a trust signal in itself: address, hours, area
   served (whole province, mainly the south), phone, Messenger. — fills from: Cluster 1
   (location) + Cluster 5 Q19 (hours) — action: visit / call.

**Why this order:** legitimacy/accountability is the reason anyone chooses Jennys over a
cheaper or closer yard, so the page *opens* on it and returns to it (§3) rather than
burying it in an "About." Range comes early (§2) because installers — the #1 audience —
scan for what's stocked. The family/resilience story (§4) earns its place as proof of
"real people," not as a vanity bio. Practical buying terms (§5) precede the CTA because
trade buyers decide once they know the terms. Everything lands on the quote.

## Bespoke sections (why this isn't a template)
- **Someone to answer for the work** — the accountability/legitimacy promise as a
  first-class block (the real differentiator, in its legally-safe form).
- **Built by a family, still here** — the family-founded origin + resilient comeback.
- **How buying works** — downpayment/terms/cash/delivery-days: concrete local mechanics
  a stock template never carries.

# Jennys Glass & Aluminum — Essence Brief

## The one sentence
For the installers and homeowners of Antique who need glass and aluminum done
right — a family-run, fully-permitted yard that has supplied, fabricated, and
installed for eight years, so there is always a real store and real people who
answer for the job.

## Audience emotional state
- **Arrives feeling:** wary of being left with no one to answer for the work — a
  homeowner unsure who's accountable behind a freelance installer; an installer
  needing to know the stock is real and the store will stand behind them.
- **Leaves feeling:** reassured there's a legitimate, established business behind
  the transaction — confident enough to call or message for a quote.
- **Trust required:** **high** — real money up front (50% down), someone's home or
  storefront on the line, and accountability is literally the product. Not
  health/very-high, but clearly above ordinary retail.

## Vibe axes (a point on each, 1–10)
- **Warm 4** — family-built (to fund the owner's children's education and care for
  staff), personal accountability, aftercare. Warm-leaning and human; not a cozy
  lifestyle brand and not the cold "expensive/authoritative" register of the prior
  template. Practicality is handled by *density*, not by going cold.
- **Serious 7** — real money, materials, and accountability for a working
  yard recovering from hardship; the trade audience and the stakes push serious,
  while the family warmth keeps it off the grim-corporate extreme.
- **Dense 4** — a genuine range (glass · aluminum · roll-up + accessories, plus
  fabrication and installation) that installers scan fast; denser than middle so
  the breadth reads clearly, without becoming a SKU catalog.
- **Indie 4** — family-run, local, personal accountability, a comeback story → the
  *character* is indie. Its superpower, though, is institutional-grade legitimacy
  (permits, receipts, physical store, 8 years), so credentials must be surfaced
  loudly as **proof**, even while the overall register stays family-local. (This
  is the axis the prior template got most wrong — it went full corporate.)
- **Analog 3** — physical materials, a physical store, cash transactions, a
  hands-on trade. Hands-on and real, not tech-forward; the future online-quotation
  idea is a mild aspiration only.
- **Quiet 6** — wins on trust and accountability, explicitly **not** on price, so
  reassurance over promotion → quieter than a deal-driven discount yard; still
  needs to actively drive "get a quote," so not silent.

## Five keywords
accountable · legitimate · established · family-run · grounded

## What this is NOT
- **Not** cold, corporate, or "expensive/authoritative" (the prior template's
  misread — architect-targeted premium gloss).
- **Not** salesy or discount/deal-driven — they don't compete on being cheapest.
- **Not** a faceless big-box (Home Depot) — the whole point is real people and a
  real store.
- **Not** stiff, clinical, or template-generic.
- **Not** a slick "futuristic tech" brand it isn't — and **must not** state the
  legally-sensitive mechanism (installers using the store's permit/name for
  credibility). Express only the legal version: a permitted, accountable store
  that stands behind every job.

## Aesthetic precedents
- **Closest match:** a well-run, long-standing local trade counter — the kind with
  the business permit framed on the wall and a person who knows you — crossed with
  the reassurance of a trusted family doctor's office (someone real, accountable,
  who picks up).
- **Wrong direction:** a glossy big-box chain, or a cold premium-architectural
  supplier site (i.e. the existing `DESIGN.md` "Trust & Heritage" direction).
- **Off-screen reference:** *the store itself* — a physical counter where the
  permit and official receipts are visible, you talk to a real person, and you
  know exactly who to call back. This single image encodes the whole brief:
  legitimacy + person + place. The orchestrator should lean on it hardest.

## Constraints
- **Platform:** web (mobile-first — many trade/home buyers arrive via phone and
  Facebook Messenger).
- **Density:** content-moderate, action-oriented — present the material/service
  range clearly, but funnel toward a quote.
- **Accessibility floor:** WCAG AA.
- **Locale:** Province of Antique, Philippines · currency **PHP** · **Facebook
  Messenger is a primary contact channel**, alongside phone and walk-in.
- **Brand guardrails:** logo colours are the fixed point. A deep navy was extracted
  from the logo in the prior pass — **verify against the actual logo file before
  locking** (logo not re-confirmed in this discovery).

## Archetype-fit note (for the orchestrator's Match step — do not decide here)
Reads **warm + grounded + analog**, family-local in character but
**institutional-by-proof** (legitimacy is the differentiator). It is **not** Cold
Precision Grotesk (that was the prior misread) and **not** Kinetic Maximalist
(not loud/futurist). Closest built pole is **Warm Editorial Scrapbook with
playfulness dialed hard down and seriousness/groundedness raised** — but the
trade-supplier register plus the heavy need to *display credentials* (permits,
receipts, years, physical store) may exceed what Scrapbook does naturally. Strong
candidate for the **archetype-synthesizer**, likely toward the reserved
**"Institutional Authority"** slot or a grounded **local-trade / family-establishment**
one-off. Whatever is chosen: **do not repeat the cold corporate-premium direction.**
